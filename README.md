## About Me 

I am an **Aeronautics PhD student** at Imperial College London, part of the **Computational Astrodynamics (COAST) group**. My research focuses on autonomous sensing and tracking of Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellites under severe uncertainty 🛰️.

My research interests span **orbit determination (OD), uncertainty quantification (UQ), machine learning (ML), and space exploration**. I am particularly interested in developing computational frameworks that solve challenging scientific and engineering problems.

## Previous Projects 

### 🧬 Machine Learning for Biosignature Detection - Imperial College London
Developed `mlbiosig`, an interpretable ML framework for distinguishing between biological and non-biological py-GC-MS chemical samples in icy ocean world environments, in support of NASA's Europa Clipper-MASPEX instrument. 

[![mlbiosig](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-mlbiosig-FF1493?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Gabriella8080/mlbiosig)

**Publication:** *Rajpoot, G. M. Z., et al. (2026). [From Mass Spectra to Biosignatures: Interpretable Machine Learning for Life Detection on Icy Ocean Worlds](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=y50tZ6zJLj). NeurIPS 2026 Interp4Discovery Workshop.*

**Research interests:** Machine learning · Explainable AI · SHAP · Astrobiology · Biosignature detection

### 📡 Characterising LEO Satellite Interference - University of Cambridge
Developed `astrotrack`, a Python package for characterising LEO satellite interference in radio observations using TLE/SGP4 propagation and site geometry, in support of the Cambridge-led REACH experiment in South Africa. 

[![astrotrack](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-astrotrack-FF1493?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/Gabriella8080/astrotrack)

**Publication:** *Rajpoot, G. M. Z., et al. astrotrack: Characterising LEO Satellite Interference in Wide-Field Radio
Astronomy. RAS Techniques & Instruments (in preparation).*

**Research interests:** Satellite tracking · Radio astronomy · Radio-frequency interference · Orbital mechanics
